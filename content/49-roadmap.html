Eight weeks at about six hours a week takes you from "I only use chat apps" to training, steering and uncensoring your own models. Weeks 1 to 4 build the foundations. Weeks 5 to 8 are about training and editing. Every week ends with something you build. Tick boxes as you go. They are remembered in this browser.

A typical week

Session 1 (about 2 h): read the first lessons slowly. Session 2 (about 2 h): do the hands-on steps. Session 3 (about 2 h): finish the lessons, answer every "Check yourself" question, and write up the deliverable.

Phase 1: Foundations

Week 1: Concepts and your PC

Deliverable: NOTES.md with your GPU, VRAM, driver, the size class you will start with, and token counts for five texts.

Week 2: Model files and your first model

Deliverable: predicted vs measured VRAM and tokens per second for one model at three settings.

Week 3: Mastery and model choice

Deliverable: a ten-prompt shootout between two models, including one uncensored variant if you wish.

Week 4: Use it for real

Deliverable: ask.py, a RAG index of your notes answering with citations, and tools_demo.py working.

Phase 2: Training and editing

Week 5: How models work and customizing

Deliverable: the gradient-descent script run at three learning rates, and one custom Modelfile model.

Week 6: Data and your first fine-tune

Deliverable: 200+ reviewed examples, a trained adapter, and a run log.

Week 7: Evaluate, export, go further

Deliverable: your fine-tune running in LM Studio, with an A/B score against the base model.

Week 8: Steering, abliteration and the capstone

Deliverable: an edited model with before-and-after measurements, and a capstone spec.
