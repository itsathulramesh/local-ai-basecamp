You already use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. This course takes you from there to running, understanding, training and customising open-weight models on your own Windows PC with an NVIDIA graphics card, including making your own uncensored variants. It assumes no AI knowledge and only basic Python.

Where you will end up

By the end you will be able to:

  • Explain how a language model works, from tokens to the transformer to training.
  • Pick models that fit your graphics card, run them in LM Studio and Ollama, and tune every setting.
  • Use local models from your own Python code, with structured output, tools and your own documents.
  • Fine-tune a model on your own data with LoRA, evaluate it honestly, and export it to GGUF.
  • Change a model's behaviour by steering and by editing its weights, including abliteration, and build your own uncensored custom model.

How each lesson works

  • A short list of what you will be able to do at the end.
  • Concept boxes hold the one idea worth remembering. Say it back in your own words.
  • On your PC boxes tell you what to click or type on Windows.
  • Calculators and demos are live. Change the numbers and watch what moves.
  • Scripts are complete and can be copied with one click. Run them; do not just read them.
  • Every lesson ends with a short "Check yourself" quiz. Try to answer before you read the explanation. Retrieval practice is the most effective study method there is.

How to study

  1. Make a folder called ai-lab in your user folder and a file in it called NOTES.md. Write down your GPU, your numbers and your surprises. You will use these notes in later lessons.
  2. Do the hands-on steps on your own machine. Reading about VRAM is not the same as watching it fill up.
  3. Change one thing at a time. If you change the model and the context length together you learn nothing.
  4. Expect to be confused for a while. Terms like "quantization" sound intimidating and turn out to be simple. The glossary and search (Ctrl+K) are one click away.
  5. When something breaks, write down the exact error. That is the start of fixing it.

A note on dates

Local AI moves fast. Specific model names, versions and app screens in this course reflect November 2026. The ideas (memory math, file formats, sampling, LoRA, directions) do not change. When a model name here looks old, search for the newest model in the same size class and apply the same method.

Check yourself

Why does this course start with concepts and hardware instead of training?

  1. Training is not possible on Windows
  2. Training is less important than chatting
  3. Training decisions (model size, VRAM, templates, evaluation) depend on understanding how models and your hardware work

Every choice in module 9 builds on modules 1 to 7.

What is the best use of the "Check yourself" questions?

  1. Try to answer before reading the explanation
  2. Skip them if you understood the lesson
  3. Read the answers first so you do not get anything wrong

Trying to recall an answer, even when you get it wrong, strengthens memory far more than rereading.
