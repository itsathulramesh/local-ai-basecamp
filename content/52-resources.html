Good places to go deeper. All are free to read unless noted.

Tools

  • LM Studio: desktop app and documentation for local models
  • Ollama: command-line model runner and model library
  • llama.cpp: the engine under LM Studio and Ollama, and the home of GGUF
  • Hugging Face: models, datasets and docs
  • PyTorch install selector
  • Unsloth docs: fine-tuning guides, per-model notebooks and VRAM tables
  • TRL SFTTrainer and PEFT: the libraries under Unsloth
  • llama.cpp quantize guide: converting and quantizing
  • mergekit: model merging
  • WSL installation guide
  • NVIDIA driver downloads

Learning

  • Local AI Lab by Sethu: much of this course's material on how models work, training and weight editing is adapted from it and rewritten for Windows and NVIDIA
  • 3Blue1Brown: Neural networks: the best visual introduction, including how transformers work
  • Andrej Karpathy on YouTube: "Intro to Large Language Models" and "Let's build GPT"
  • Hugging Face Learn: free courses on LLMs and Transformers
  • r/LocalLLaMA: the community for local models. Useful, but verify claims.

Papers mentioned

  • Arditi et al. (2024), Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction: the idea behind abliteration
  • Vaswani et al. (2017), Attention Is All You Need: the transformer architecture
  • Hu et al. (2021), LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models
  • Dettmers et al. (2023), QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs
  • Zhou et al. (2023), LIMA: Less Is More for Alignment: quality over quantity in fine-tuning data
  • Rafailov et al. (2023), Direct Preference Optimization
  • Qi et al. (2023), Fine-tuning Aligned Language Models Compromises Safety, Even When Users Do Not Intend To!
  • Meng et al. (2022), Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT (ROME)
  • Ilharco et al. (2023), Editing Models with Task Arithmetic
  • Maxime Labonne (2024), Uncensor any LLM with abliteration: tutorial with the healing result in lesson 10.3

Sources used for this course's figures

  • LM Studio documentation (lmstudio.ai/docs): server, API and CLI
