Habits that keep you improving

  • Keep your run log and model cards. Your own results are the best teacher.
  • Follow new releases in your size class and retest your capstone on them. A newer base model often beats more training.
  • Read the model card and the Unsloth notebook for every new model family before training it.
  • Re-measure after every tool update: LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp and Unsloth change monthly.
  • Join the community (r/LocalLLaMA, Hugging Face discussions) and verify claims yourself.
